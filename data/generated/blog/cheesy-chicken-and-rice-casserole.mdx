Cheesy chicken and rice casserole is a Tuesday night dinner. It's the kind of kid-friendly meal that goes from fridge to table in 35 minutes with one baking dish and a single skillet, and it's one of those family-friendly meals where everyone actually eats what's on their plate without you having to broker a deal.

## Why This Recipe Works

- **Realistic 35 minutes.** Ten minutes of prep, 25 minutes in the oven. That's it.
- **The rice cooks in the dish.** No separate pot. No draining. One less thing.
- **Kids eat it.** The cheesy sauce coats everything — vegetables included. This is how peas disappear.
- **Flexible.** Swap the protein, change the vegetable, use what's already in your kitchen.
- **Leftovers hold up.** Reheats well the next day with a splash of broth to loosen it up.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 cup long-grain white rice, uncooked
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 can (10.5 oz) cream of chicken soup
- 1.5 cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided
- 1 cup frozen peas or broccoli florets
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Lightly grease a 9x13 baking dish.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together chicken broth, cream of chicken soup, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper until smooth.
3. Stir in the uncooked rice and 1 cup of the shredded cheddar. Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish.
4. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken pieces and sear 2-3 minutes until lightly browned on the outside — they don't need to be cooked through at this point.
5. Nestle the seared chicken pieces into the rice mixture. Scatter frozen peas or broccoli over the top.
6. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 20 minutes.
7. Remove foil, sprinkle the remaining 1/2 cup cheddar over the top, and bake uncovered for 5 more minutes until the cheese is melted and starting to bubble at the edges.
8. Let it rest 5 minutes before serving — the rice finishes absorbing liquid as it sits, and cutting into it immediately will make it soupy.

## Tips and Substitutions

- **Swap the protein.** Boneless chicken thighs work great here and stay a little juicier. Rotisserie chicken also works — skip the searing step and stir it straight into the rice mixture, then reduce bake time by 5 minutes.
- **Change the vegetable.** Frozen corn, diced carrots, or chopped green beans all fit naturally. If you use fresh broccoli instead of frozen, cut it small so it softens in time.
- **Make it gluten-free.** Use a gluten-free cream of chicken soup (Pacific Foods makes one) and double-check your broth label. Everything else in this recipe is naturally gluten-free.
- **Cheese options.** Sharp cheddar gives the most flavor, but Monterey Jack melts smoother if you have kids who are picky about texture. A mix of both is not a bad call.
- **Prep ahead.** Mix the broth, soup, and seasoning the night before and refrigerate. The next evening you're just searing chicken and assembling — closer to 20 minutes of active work.
- **Honest note on leftovers.** The broccoli gets a little soft after reheating. If that bothers you, use peas instead — they hold up better. Either way, reheat with a tablespoon or two of broth stirred in to keep the rice from drying out.
